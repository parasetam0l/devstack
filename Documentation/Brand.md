# DevStack identity

The app uses a simple solid teal stack on a matte navy tile, with a white terminal glyph. Liquid Glass belongs to the interface controls and navigation. Content panels stay opaque for readability. The template menu bar symbol repeats the three layers and terminal glyph.

Final asset: `Sources/DevStackApp/Resources/DevStackIcon.png`. The icon set is generated with `scripts/build-icon.sh`.

Generation mode: built-in image generation, editing the earlier stack concept, transparent background.

Final prompt:

> Redesign this DevStack app icon to be simple, restrained, crisp and professional. Keep only the central idea of three stacked square layers and a small >_ terminal glyph on the top layer, with a dark navy macOS rounded-square tile. Completely remove all glass, transparency, refraction, glow, sparkle, iridescence, decorative reflections and glossy highlights. Three solid matte teal layers, subtly separated in isometric perspective. Flat opaque surfaces, exceptionally clean geometry, softly rounded corners, almost vector-like simplicity, a quiet minimal soft shadow only to distinguish layers. White terminal glyph on top. Navy tile must be matte and uniform. Balanced generous negative space; stack occupies 60 percent of icon, tile 86 percent of square canvas. Design should read perfectly at 32px. Think sober beautifully crafted developer utility, not a luxury glass sculpture. No gradients except a barely perceptible shading difference between top/front faces. No extra text, no D monogram, no frame, no mockup. Outside rounded-square tile fully transparent with clean antialiased edge. ONE standalone final icon.
